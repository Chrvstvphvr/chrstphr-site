Yeah, that's a fair question.

Why a vending machine?+
Because most "skills" pages are a bullet list with no information density. You can vend a quarter and get exactly the same insight. This way at least you have to push a button.
What's the difference between "Expert" and "Fluent"?+
Expert means CHRSTPHR has shipped it under pressure, mentored on it, and has strong opinions about how it should be done. Fluent means working in it confidently and shipping — but still actively learning. Exploring means playing with it but you'd hire someone better than CHRSTPHR to lead it.
Where's the pricing?+
Not on this page. This is a skills page, not a menu. Pricing happens after we figure out if CHRSTPHR is the right person for what you actually need.
Are you actively doing all 12 of these?+
At any given moment? No. Across a year of projects? Pretty much, yeah. Most projects pull on 3–5 of these at once.
What about skills NOT in the machine?+
Then either CHRSTPHR doesn't do it, or isn't good enough at it to put it on display. Better to decline a project than fake it.

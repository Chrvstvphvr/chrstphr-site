SUBJECT / 1.0 CHRSTPHR ●
★
HUMAN · DESIGNER · OPINIONATED REC

I'm CHRSTPHR — designer, professional vowel skipper, and someone who believes interfaces should make you feel something. Even if that something is "wait, what?"

I've spent the last decade-ish bouncing between UX/UI for actual products, brand systems with strong opinions, and weird little AI experiments where I see how far I can push the tools before they fight back.

I don't believe in "clean and minimal" as a personality. I believe in clarity, attitude, and surprise — in roughly that order. The best work is the kind people screenshot to their friends.

If you want a pixel-pusher who'll polish your dashboard into oblivion, you're in the wrong place. If you want someone who'll ask weird questions, push back on the brief, and ship something with bite — hi.

— CHRSTPHR
Currently

Designing tools that behave like collaborators, not interfaces.

Mostly AI-native UX, brand systems, and the occasional ridiculous side project.
